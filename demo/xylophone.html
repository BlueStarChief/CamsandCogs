Eight-Bar Xylophone

Bar lengths are worked out from beam physics, and the bars are set on an arc around the player's turning axis. The table gives the angle he has to turn to for each note, and how precisely he has to land. Angles are measured from straight ahead: minus is to his left (low notes), plus to his right. Click a bar or a row to hear it.

Where he turns, and the bar cutting list

The layout SVG is a full-size template: every bar outline with its two cord holes, the turning axis and the mallet arc. Print it at 100% to place the bars, or send it to a laser cutter (red = cut, blue = engrave). Cut metal bars about 3% long and file the ends to bring them up to pitch. Wood varies, so cut it 5% long and tune by thinning the middle underneath, which lowers the note.
